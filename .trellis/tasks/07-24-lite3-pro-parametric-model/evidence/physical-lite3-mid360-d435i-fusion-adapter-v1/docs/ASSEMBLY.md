# Assembly Sequence Represented by the V1 Animation

> **Superseded for the purchased current Lite3 Pro.** The robot-side steps,
> four deck locations, spacers, and screw lengths below belong to the rejected
> V1 digital scene. Do not execute them on Dr Sun's robot. Only the off-robot
> upper sensor-module order is retained as reusable sequence evidence.

## Boundary

This sequence proves only that the modeled rigid parts, fasteners, and driver
corridors can be ordered without intentional pass-through. It does not set
torque, threadlocker, final screw lengths, or authorize physical installation.
The monolithic carrier replaces the former lower/upper-bracket joint, so there
are no hidden inter-layer bolts or nuts in V1.

## Off-robot module assembly

1. Start with the bare one-piece carrier.
2. Seat the D435i directly against the two short camera faces.
3. Install its two M3 screws independently from the camera side. The animation
   shows the active screw and hex key in close-up before upper geometry exists.
4. Seat Mid-360 on the source 48 x 36 mm four-point interface.
5. Install the four underside M3 screws one at a time in a cross pattern.
6. Lower the S410 guard over Mid-360 without passing it through the sensor.
7. Install four independent guard screws in a cross pattern into the
   J20A-derived receiver axes. V1 models direct/tapped receivers and no loose
   guard nuts; the final printed-thread or metal-insert strategy remains a
   coupon decision.
8. Hold the completed sensor module off the robot.

## Rejected V1 module-to-Lite3 sequence

9. Place the two rear locating spacers on the rear physical deck seats while
   the complete module remains 100 mm above the robot.
10. Lower the complete module vertically onto all four deck locations while
    retaining clearance from the white Interface enclosure.
11. Install the four Lite3 screws independently in a diagonal cross order:
    front-left, rear-right, front-right, rear-left.
12. Inspect the local joint, cable exits, sensor windows, guard clearance, and
    then the complete standing Lite3 from a global view.

## Mandatory current-Pro redesign gate

Measure the current-Pro receivers and compute-enclosure keep-outs, then design
a separate lower adapter and a new human-accessible sequence. Do not repair
this sequence by substituting another guessed hole pattern. Physical proof-load
and moving-robot work remain outside V1.
