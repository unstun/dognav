# Current Lite3 Pro D435i support study Rev B

This package rebuilds the low-front D435i support candidate against the
scan-registered Rev B compute keep-out and the source-backed J20A/MID-360/S410
upper stack. The support is a single valid solid and preserves the official
45 mm rear M3 axis pitch.

The two lower pads remain future union geometry. They do not define a Lite3
receiver, hole, thread, screw length, or load path. The D435i is represented by
its official nominal collision envelope in this headless check; the detailed
manufacturer B-rep remains in the Fusion review archive.
