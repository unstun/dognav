`rejected_d435_late_collision`

Dr Sun rejected this revision because D435i was installed after J20/MID360.
That late insertion forces the camera or its two screw/tool paths through the
already installed upper geometry.  The off-robot bottom-fastener and two-stage
carrier-transfer logic remains useful, but this sequence is not current.

Replacement:

`../factory-step-full-installation-animation-d435-first-human-sequence/lite3-complete-installation-animation-d435-first-no-text.mp4`

The superseded order was:

1. seat MID360 on J20 away from Lite3 and install all four underside screws;
2. pre-insert the four bottom-up J17/J20 bolts while J20 is still separate;
3. lower the J20/MID360 subassembly over the protruding bolts;
4. install the accessible top nuts, washers, and locknuts;
5. seat the rear locating spacers on Lite3;
6. align the completed carrier outside the robot envelope, then approach the
   mounting surface along its normal;
7. install the exposed front and rear base screws;
8. install D435i and its two outward-accessible screws last.

Its other access checks do not override the rejected D435i step.
