`rejected_unreasonable_s410_fastening_animation`

The official manufacturer S410 BRep is included as a separately installed
guard after MID360 is secured to J20. Its four 5.2 mm clearance holes align
to J20's four M5 through-thread axes. Four M5x8 socket-head screws are shown
as visual candidates because the public drawings do not establish the
supplied screw length, head standard, torque, or thread engagement.

The source assembly transform left all four guard axes offset from the matching
J20 axes by the same 0.1708495 mm in-plane vector. The candidate preserves both
manufacturer BReps and applies one rigid in-plane translation to S410 and its
four screws; no source body is scaled, stretched, or remodeled.

The video remains a visual assembly-order and gross-access demonstration. It
does not validate wrench clearance, tolerance, structural strength, vibration,
or physical-installation safety.

The 30-second, 720-frame, no-text MP4 and the corrected 28-occurrence F3D are
rendered, hashed, and validated. The task remains open until Dr Sun visually
accepts this candidate.

Dr Sun rejected this candidate because the four S410 screws move together as
one rigid group and only translate into the holes. It does not demonstrate a
human-executable, individually rotated fastening sequence. Preserve it as
negative evidence and do not present it as the current animation.
