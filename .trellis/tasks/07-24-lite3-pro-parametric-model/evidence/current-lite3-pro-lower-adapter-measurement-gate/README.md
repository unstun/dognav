# Current Lite3 Pro Lower-Adapter Measurement Gate

This package is the input boundary for the replacement lower adapter. Ten
physical tape-measure photographs from Dr Sun's purchased current Lite3 Pro
now establish a `65.0 +/- 1.0 mm` front small-hole axis pitch and a forward-deck
longitudinal scaffold. The registered physical scan upgrades the compute-box
plan view from the early rectangular photo proxy to a two-front-recess nominal
footprint. Collision screening still uses a separate expanded rectangular
keep-out. The original photos and hashes are preserved under `source/`, and the
readings and uncertainty are recorded in `measurement_results.json`.

It still does not contain a printable lower adapter. The photographs do not
prove thread designation, usable depth, receiver material, the structural role
of the visible centre candidate, or the remaining cable/vent/service sweeps.

The future part will remain separate from the Mid-360/D435i/S410 upper module,
place that module ahead of the compute enclosure, expose every robot screw to a
human tool, and keep the enclosure feet, ventilation, connectors, and cables
clear.

Fill the remaining null receiver fields in `parameters.json` from the purchased robot.
`receiver_measurement_request.json` is the machine-readable A/B/C/D request,
and `renders/current-lite3-pro-receiver-measurement-card-rev-b.png` is the
matching Chinese visual card. A/B are the two front small holes, C is the
unverified centre feature, and D is the enclosure service/cable sweep.
The legacy `74 x 94 mm` Pro pattern, Venture/J17A robot-side pattern, and V1
Experience-style axes are recorded only as rejected comparison values.
