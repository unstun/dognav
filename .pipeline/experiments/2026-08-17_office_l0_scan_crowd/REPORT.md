Two 60 s, same-input runs completed the project Foxy SCAN closed loop in the
official Office L0 scene. Both runs reached the final goal, had no non-foot
collision, protocol error, reconnect, sequence gap, or watchdog event, and
contained at least one sensor-causal SCAN B-spline replacement associated with
a crossing pedestrian.

This establishes project `integrated` evidence and validates the declared
single Office simulation scenario against its automated gate. It is not an
upstream SCAN reproduction, real MID-360/D435i validation, real Lite3 evidence,
predictive dynamic planning, or social-navigation validation. AC55 is entirely
human-owned and is not satisfied by this report.

```text
sparse waypoints -> Foxy SCAN -> cmd_vel -> TCP bridge v1
  -> V12 model_149999 policy -> articulated Lite3 + PhysX
  -> simulated LiDAR/depth + simulator-truth pose -> Foxy SCAN
```

The dense AABB route is used only for preflight and evaluation. Runtime local
trajectories are generated by SCAN. Person ground-truth circles in the review
overlay are explicitly labeled `GT pedestrian routes (evaluation only)` and do
not populate SCAN or steer the robot.

## 4. Causal audit

A plan publication is counted as pedestrian-reactive only when all four links
are present for the same named pedestrian:

1. per-person LiDAR and depth detection;
2. the walking person approaches the remaining continuous active B-spline;
3. an actually captured `/grid_map/occupancy_inflate` sample is near that person;
4. a causally later, same-target B-spline has a measurable geometry change.

ROS receipt times are mapped to simulator time by bracketing body-pose receipts
and interpolating their simulator stamps. This replaced the invalid assumption
that wall-clock deltas represent simulator-time deltas while Isaac runs slower
than real time.

- Candidate 38 contains five qualifying replacements caused by `crossing_2`.
  Geometric changes range from 0.111 m to 0.552 m.
- Candidate 39 contains one qualifying replacement caused by `crossing_1`:
  LiDAR 43 hits, depth 219 pixels, active-path distance 1.171 m, captured SCAN
  inflated-occupancy distance 0.004 m, and later B-spline change 0.161 m.

Other trajectory publications remain labeled startup, waypoint progression, or
untriggered; they are not inflated into the reactive count.

## 13. R2.0.1 live-cloud and transfer-delivery reliability preflight

`office-r2.0.1-preflight` is a one-change-group instrumentation and delivery
patch over `office-r2.0.0-preflight`. The confirmed display defect was a clock
domain mismatch: genuine MID-360 scans remain 10 Hz in simulator time, while
the approximately 0.051 real-time factor makes successive arrivals several
wall-clock seconds apart. RViz's former 0.4 s decay therefore expired the last
cloud before the next real cloud arrived. The repair sets only the native
`Live LiDAR Cloud` display decay to zero, whose Foxy meaning is to retain the
latest points until replaced; it does not alter the sensor schedule, pattern,
range, ray count, timestamps, planner, route, policy, robot, or pedestrians.

The live-mode audit is now observation-only and fail-closed. It subscribes to
the same-run `/quad_0/cloud`, requires at least one real observation, checks
nonempty clouds and strictly increasing stamps, separates simulator-time gaps
from recorded wall-clock gaps, and verifies the delivered combined video rather
than inferring visibility from ROS receipt alone. A native-RViz prestart gate
pauses Isaac until the original same-container subscriber is ready, removing a
short-run startup measurement race without changing any simulated timing.
